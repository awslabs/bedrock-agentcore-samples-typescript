# Demo Script: Digital Twin Integration with FLS PAL

## Overview

This expanded demo story version shows how modern refineries use digital twin technology for early warning of equipment issues, which then feeds into maintenance management systems like FLS PAL. It demonstrates the full circle of digital transformation in industrial operations - from prediction to planning to execution.

---

## Prologue: The Digital Warning

**Setting:** Control Room of Gulf Coast Refinery, 4:45 AM

*The refinery's digital twin platform suddenly highlights a critical Centrifugal Pump in red on the main display. Multiple health indicators are flashing, showing unusual vibration patterns and declining performance metrics.*

**DIGITAL TWIN AGENT:** "Alert: Anomaly detected in Centrifugal Pump P-2201. Vibration analysis indicates bearing deterioration beyond acceptable parameters. Current efficiency at 72% and declining."

*The system displays a trend graph showing accelerating deterioration over the past 48 hours.*

**DIGITAL TWIN AGENT:** "Historical analysis suggests high probability (89%) of bearing failure within the next 72 hours if left unaddressed. Recommended action: Immediate inspection and maintenance planning."

*Control room alarms begin sounding.*

**OPERATOR JONES:** *(checking DCS screens)* "We're getting high vibration alarms on P-2201. Temperature differential is also out of spec. Better get this logged as an emergency work order."

**DIGITAL TWIN AGENT:** "Based on current operating conditions and historical maintenance records, recommended actions:
1. Reduce flow by 15% to minimize immediate risk
2. Schedule emergency inspection within next 12 hours
3. Prepare for bearing replacement within 48 hours
4. Estimated maintenance duration: 6-8 hours
5. Required resources: 2 mechanics, 1 instrument tech"

*The agent automatically generates a detailed work order with all relevant technical documentation, historical maintenance records, and safety procedures.*

**OPERATOR JONES:** *(picking up the phone)* "I better call Walt about this one. He'll need to coordinate with his FLS PAL to get this sorted."

---

## Scene 1: The Morning Crisis

**Setting:** Walt's office, 5:30 AM

*Walt Mayfield, looking stressed, enters his office.*

**WALT:** *(sighing)* "Another day, another pile of emergencies. Let's see what we've got..." *(checking his phone)* "Great, an emergency work order for P-2201. Just what I need first thing in the morning..."

**WALT:** "Three emergency work orders from last night, and we're already behind on regulatory maintenance. We're also at the top of our budget allocation with little extra money to spare. How am I supposed to juggle all this?"

*Walt logs into FLS PAL, which has already received and analyzed the digital twin alert.*

**FLS PAL:** "Good morning, Walt. I've received the emergency work order for P-2201. I've analyzed the digital twin data and verified the urgency. I've also integrated all three emergency work orders from last night with your existing schedule. Here's a summary of today's priorities and suggested adjustments."

**WALT:** *(surprised)* "Wow, that's... comprehensive. And it's already factored in the cost impacts?"

**FLS PAL:** "Yes, Walt. I've calculated the OPEX impacts of both immediate repairs and potential deferred maintenance. Based on this analysis, I recommend addressing two of the three emergency requests immediately, while the third can be safely scheduled for tomorrow without significant risk or cost increase."

**WALT:** *(impressed)* "That's incredibly helpful. What about our regulatory maintenance?"

**FLS PAL:** "I've adjusted the schedule to accommodate the urgent work while still keeping us on track for regulatory compliance. Here's a breakdown of resource allocation and a draft of safety briefings for each team."

**WALT:** *(smiling)* "This is going to make the 6 AM operations meeting so much smoother."

---

## Scene 2: Walt's Improved Day with FLS PAL

**Setting:** In the field

*Walt is wearing a hard hat and using his mobile device.*

**WALT:** *(speaking into his device)* "FLS PAL, update status on the P-2201 Centrifugal Pump repair."

**FLS PAL:** "The bearing replacement is 75% complete, Walt. I've coordinated with the electrical team for final testing, and they'll be ready in approximately 45 minutes. Vibration readings from the digital twin are already showing improvement on the partially completed work."

**WALT:** "Great. Any other updates?"

**FLS PAL:** "Yes, there's a new emergency work request from operations regarding a pump in Unit 3. I've evaluated its urgency against our standardized criteria. It can be addressed this afternoon without impacting the P-2201 completion or our other scheduled work. Would you like me to make the necessary adjustments?"

**WALT:** *(nodding)* "Yes, please do. And inform all relevant stakeholders."

**FLS PAL:** "Understood. I'll update the schedule, generate safety briefings, and send automated updates to operations and craft teams. The system will also provide real-time cost implications of these changes."

**WALT:** *(to a passing worker)* "Hey, Jim! How's your team doing with the regulatory work on the distillation column?"

**JIM:** "Actually ahead of schedule, Walt. Having clear, uninterrupted time to focus on the job has made a huge difference, especially since FLS PAL helped us prioritize around that P-2201 emergency."

**WALT:** *(grinning)* "That's what I like to hear!"

---

## Scene 3: End of Day Reflection

**Setting:** Walt's office, end of day

*Walt is back in his office, looking relaxed.*

**WALT:** "FLS PAL, give me a summary of today's accomplishments."

**FLS PAL:** "Certainly, Walt. Today, we successfully completed the emergency repair on P-2201, with the digital twin confirming normal operation parameters. The early detection helped us prevent an unplanned shutdown. We also completed another emergency repair, made significant progress on three regulatory maintenance tasks, and improved our craft utilization rate by 22% compared to last week's average. We're currently projecting to be 5% ahead of our monthly maintenance schedule."

**WALT:** *(leaning back in his chair, satisfied)* "Incredible. You know, FLS PAL, I used to spend my days putting out fires and constantly falling behind. That P-2201 situation would have thrown our whole schedule into chaos. Now, I'm actually getting ahead and making real improvements. This is a game-changer for our refinery."

**FLS PAL:** "I'm glad to help, Walt. The integration with the digital twin system has significantly improved our ability to predict and prevent equipment failures. Is there anything else you need assistance with?"

**WALT:** "No, that's all for today. But I'm looking forward to seeing what we can accomplish tomorrow. Together, we're making this refinery safer, more efficient, and more productive than ever before."

*Walt shuts down his computer and leaves the office with a spring in his step.*
